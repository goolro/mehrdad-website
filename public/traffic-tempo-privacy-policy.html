Privacy Policy — Traffic Tempo

Last updated: September 2026 — App version: 3.4.0

1) What data we collect

Traffic Tempo collects the minimum data possible:

  • Daily Challenge score: to show the leaderboard, only a "display name", the "score" and the "date" are stored on Google Firebase (Firestore).
  • Install identifier: a push-notification token (FCM Token) is kept on the same service to deliver the daily reminder.
  • Game progress: levels, stars and sound settings are stored only on your own phone and are never uploaded.

2) What we do NOT collect

  • No real name, no e-mail, no phone number, no location
  • No advertising identifiers and no cross-app tracking data
  • We never sell any data to any third party

3) App permissions

  • Notifications (Android 13+, only with your consent): to show the daily challenge reminder
  • Internet: to submit your leaderboard score and receive reminders — everything else works fully offline
  • Vibration: tactile feedback for moves

4) Data deletion

To have your leaderboard score completely removed, just send us an e-mail; it will be deleted within 7 days at the latest. Uninstalling the app also wipes all on-device data.

5) Third-party service

Google Firebase (Firestore + Cloud Messaging) — governed by the Firebase Privacy & Security policy.
