Angels don't fund ideas.
They fund evidence.

Anyone can write "TAM: $10B" on slide 4. What investors actually look for is harder to fake:

1. A founder who ships — not talks about shipping
2. A working path from problem to product that real people touch
3. Proof of execution without a 12-person team

So I stopped sending decks and started sending links: a live quiz funnel that walks investors through the thesis. A public case study. A changelog.

Edelman-LinkedIn research found 73% of decision-makers trust thought leadership content more than marketing brochures when judging a company. Translation: show your work, in public, consistently — it outperforms pitch-deck polish.

Evidence > adjectives.
