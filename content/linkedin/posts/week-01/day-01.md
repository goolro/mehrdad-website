——— از اینجا کپی کن ———

Most founders pitch investors with a deck.
I built them a quiz.

Deck = you talk for 20 minutes and hope they remember slide 9.
Quiz = the investor walks through the thesis themselves, one question at a time, and tells you where they drop off.

That is the idea behind Quiz of Koko — an interactive quiz funnel I built for the KLIKA superapp pitch. Investors self-qualify while they play. Every answer is a signal: what they care about, what they doubt, what they skip.

I am a solo product builder. My whole pitch is: I ship, on a clock, in public. This quiz is what "shipping" looks like when the product is a conversation with capital.

The full case study — what the quiz does and why a funnel beats a deck:
mehrdad.ir/case-studies/quiz-of-koko

If you are an angel who has sat through one deck too many: follow along. The next build logs will show what happens after the quiz.
