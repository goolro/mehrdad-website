"Ship every week" is not a productivity hack.
It is how you de-risk a startup before an investor even asks.

This week on my board:
→ The Strategy Session went live on mehrdad.ir — $99, 45 minutes, a written one-page memo within 24 hours
→ The AI content pipeline (three CrewAI agents on a local LLM shim) produced its first full week of brand material without a human pushing every button
→ The LinkedIn system itself: this post is part of it

I run my own build the way I run client sprints: fixed scope, weekly demo, visible changelog. Consistency is not a personality trait — it is the cheapest risk reduction a founder can offer capital.

Investors read cadence the way analysts read cash flow: weekly signals compound into trust.

What did you ship this week? One line in the comments — public accountability is free.
