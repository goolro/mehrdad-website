# PRODUCT STRATEGY — mehrdad.ir

Last updated: 2026-09-01

## Operating model

**Personal brand** — Mehrdad is the independent builder. Products are
evidence. mehrdad.ir is the personal hub and proof layer.

Each product may eventually grow its own name, brand, website, repository
and company structure — the site architecture does not force them under one
artificial company.

## Services direction (outcomes, not "web development")

Software/websites/apps are delivery mechanisms — the value proposition is a
business outcome:

- increase sales / capture leads
- automate repetitive operations
- improve customer response
- improve marketing efficiency
- analyze business/customer data
- build internal AI tools / AI-enabled products
- validate & prototype product ideas

No outcome is promised without evidence; case studies get added as they
exist in reality.

## Mobile app stance

An app is allowed **when it has a real recurring utility** (an AI tool,
assistant, saved-state workflow) — not as "Mehrdad's content" container.
When built, it appears in Work with its own honest status. The current
architecture (DB-backed content + APIs) does not block a future app.

## Investors & partners

No aggressive INVEST CTA. Serious people should be able to discover:
what Mehrdad is building → what he has already built → how he thinks →
how to contact him. Evidence first (`/work`, `/writing`), one contact funnel
(`/contact`, intent selectable including partnership/collaboration).

## Future-proofing without speculation

Foundation supports future: user accounts, saved state, APIs, newsletters,
multilingual content, multiple products. Speculative features are NOT
implemented until they have a current, real use case.
