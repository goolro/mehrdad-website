# CONTENT STRATEGY — mehrdad.ir

Last updated: 2026-09-01

## The writing is the knowledge layer

Writing is not a blog archive. It is the long-term content engine for:
search traffic, professional credibility, LinkedIn/social distribution,
partnership discovery, and future customers.

## Source of truth workflow

```
REAL WORK → REAL INSIGHT → WEBSITE ARTICLE → AI-ASSISTED REPURPOSING
→ HUMAN REVIEW → SOCIAL DISTRIBUTION
```

- The insight originates from real work (building, researching, selling,
  failing) — never from content-first generation.
- AI may assist with editing, restructuring, translation and repurposing.
- No mass-produced filler articles to fake frequency.

## What gets published

| Type | Where | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| Product decisions & build logs | Writing (→ linked to Work) | «چرا X را ساختم»، «از ساختن X چه یاد گرفتم» |
| Research | Writing (→ linked to Work) | smart city analyses, market observations |
| Experiments & failures | Writing or Lab | honest post-mortems |
| Case studies | Writing (only when measurable results exist) | — |
| Micro-notes | Lab / notes (`kind=note`) | short field notes (legacy web-stories fit here) |

## Metadata for future distribution

Every article stores structured fields that make later repurposing possible
without rewrites: `title`, `excerpt`, categories, tags (curated),
`publishedAt`, `readingMinutes`, canonical URL, related projects.
(Featured images and topic summaries to be enriched editorially over time.)

## Historical content

- The full legacy archive (82 articles, 2020–2025) ships with **restored
  original dates** — see CONTENT_MIGRATION.md.
- Historical articles are displayed as-is (light sanitization only); no
  retro-active "AI cleanup" of old text. New insight → new article.
