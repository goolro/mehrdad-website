## Post 2 — EN — Method (day 3)

Your competitor is not the other app. It's the spreadsheet. The WhatsApp group. The person who does it manually.

I ask every client the same question before writing a line of code: "What do they do today instead?"

If your product can't beat the honest alternative, it can't beat the polished competitor either.

That's question 3 of 5 in my free MVP Scope Builder — it takes 3 minutes and you keep the one-page scope:
→ mehrdad.ir/scope-builder
