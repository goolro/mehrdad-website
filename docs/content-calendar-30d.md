## Measurement (the calendar itself is evidence-gated)

- PostHog: `mvp_sprint_viewed`, `scope_builder_completed`, `playbook_subscribed` — weekly compare vs baseline week 0
- After 30 days: keep what moved, kill what didn't — same FDE loop, applied to content
- LinkedIn: track profile views + link clicks natively; do not obsess over likes

## Production notes for the owner

- Every LinkedIn post = 3–8 lines + one link. No threads in month one.
- Blog posts can start as 600–900 words. The Playbook's 10 steps are pre-written skeletons.
- Reuse: each blog post → 2–3 LinkedIn fragments (the calendar already pairs them).
- Persian posts stay Persian; English stays English (EN-purity rule).
