# BRAND STRATEGY — mehrdad.ir

Last updated: 2026-09-01

## Positioning (approved — do not replace)

> کسب‌وکار و محصولت رو با دقت طراحی می‌کنم، و با AI سریع می‌سازمش.
> **I design businesses and products with care, and build them fast with AI.**

The positioning must communicate: thoughtful business/product design, serious
product thinking, AI-assisted building, real execution, independent builder
identity.

Banned generic phrases (homepage or meta): AI Solutions, AI Innovation,
Innovation Platform, Digital Transformation, Full-Service Agency, Software
Development Agency, «طراح و پژوهشگر / Designer & Researcher» as the main
positioning.

## Brand model

**Personal brand.** Mehrdad is the identity. Products/startups/experiments/
games are evidence of what he builds. The site is not presented as a studio,
agency, or investment company — those may be earned later through real
traction and team structure.

## Three connected activities

1. **BUILD** — AI-powered products, software, tools, experiments.
2. **HELP** — help businesses use AI for measurable value (revenue, less
   repetitive work, faster response, better decisions).
3. **SHARE** — real research, decisions, failures and lessons.

One ecosystem: Research → Design → Build → Learn → Share → Build again.

## Voice & personality

intelligent · practical · independent · curious · credible · modern ·
experimental · honest · builder-oriented

Rules:

- First person singular («من») — no fake «ما/ما تیم» unless a real team exists.
- No inflated claims, fake traction, fake clients, fake metrics, fake dates.
- Unfinished projects are labeled unfinished. Experiments are labeled
  experiments. This transparency *is* the brand.
- Persian copy is natural Persian (not machine-translated), correct
  punctuation (، ؛ « »), justified body text, Vazirmatn typography.
- English copy (when it appears) is natural and short.

## Visual direction

- Independent product builder — not a freelance marketplace profile.
- Clarity > decoration. No meaningless sections, no visual complexity for
  its own sake.
- Warm neutral surfaces, single accent color, generous whitespace, strong
  hierarchy, excellent mobile experience, subtle motion only.
- RTL-first Persian layout with `Vazirmatn`.

## Anti-patterns (from the failed previous site)

- «☼» decorated titles and generic agency service grids.
- Seven parallel services (استارتاپ/فروش/هوش مصنوعی/توسعه وب/طراحی محصول/
  مشاوره/دیجیتال مارکتینگ) presented with no proof or differentiation.
- Fake-fresh dates on years-old content (the reason the date-forensics
  pipeline exists — see CONTENT_MIGRATION.md).
